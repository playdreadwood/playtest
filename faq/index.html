The playtest

When is it?

Friday, October 30, 2026, 6:30–10 PM. Stay as late as you like. The end time is just so you arrive in time.

Where is it?

Potion Portal, 2329 28th St N, St. Pete.

How long does a game take?

About 60–90 minutes. A game runs at whatever pace your table likes.

Do I need to know the rules?

No. Read How to Play if you like, or just sit down and learn at the table.

Which tier do we play?

Each table picks its own: Beginner, Deadly or Immortal. New players should start with Beginner.

How many players per table?

2 to 4. Sign-up is free with the Eventbrite RSVP, but walk-ins are welcome.

How do I pick a faction?

First come, first served. Each faction is played by one player.

How do I give feedback?

Fill out the survey after your game. About 60 seconds. Knocked out early? Do it then.

Anything else I should know?

Free parking in the lot out front. No kitchen: the bar sells snacks, and outside food or delivery is welcome. Bring nothing. The game is for ages 14+. Questions? Ask Adam. He's the one in the hat.

Turns and AP

Can I save AP for later?

No. Spend every point every turn. The one exception is a Vigilante's Vengeance AP, which can roll into your next turn (and then you spend it all).

I can't spend all my AP. Do I lose?

Not yet. Undo actions until you can spend exactly all of it. Or perish.

What does "Staked" mean?

At the start of your turn, if there's no way at all to spend exactly all your AP, you're Staked. You're out, and nobody gets credit.

When is Staked checked?

Only at the start of your turn. Mid-turn, you can always undo back to the start.

My Vampire died during my own turn. Do I still have to spend my AP?

No. The turn can end and the leftover AP is lost. A Vigilante with a second Vampire must spend it with that one.

Does turn order follow the P1–P4 labels on the layout card?

No. The labels don't matter. Sit down, take the part of the map nearest you, and play clockwise from the first player.

Who goes first?

Whoever most recently saw their own blood.

Why do I start with 6 AP but my card only goes to 5?

Players 3 and 4 get 6 AP in the first round only. Keep the extra point in your head.

Moving and pivoting

Can I move diagonally?

Never. Move one square, only the way you're facing.

Can I Pivot twice in a row?

Not with the same Vampire in the same turn. A Vigilante can pivot one Vampire and then the other. An Outlaw's Daring Escape can also pivot twice.

I ended last turn with a Pivot. Can I open this turn with one?

Yes. Pivot memory resets every turn.

Can I turn 180°?

Yes. A Pivot just has to face a new direction.

Can two Vampires share a square?

Never. One Vampire may share a square with a Tombstone, and never a Cursed one.

Shooting and cover

Can I hit my own pieces?

Yes. Shots and blasts hit friends too.

A shot hits an empty Tombstone. Then what?

It breaks and goes back to the pool. The shot stops.

My Vampire is on a Tombstone. Am I safe?

Only from a shot coming from the side you face. From any other side, you're hit and the Tombstone stays.

Does the Silver Bullet stop at a Tombstone?

Yes, unless a Bounty Hunter fired it.

What do Rampage and the Hand Cannon do to a Tombstone?

Both smash it. Rampage stops there. The Hand Cannon keeps going.

What does Under My Protection stop?

Every bullet, including Rampage, the Silver Bullet and the Hand Cannon. Never Dynamite.

Hazards

Is it a "Black Widow"?

No. That's an old name. It's the Bear Trap.

Can I throw Grave Dust at a Vampire?

No. Hazards only land on empty squares.

I'm Bleeding and step on a Bear Trap. Am I safe?

No. Your Vampire is eliminated, and nobody gets credit for it: no Vengeance and no Contract Payoff.

Which way does a Tombstone face?

It only matters when a Vampire is on it, and then it faces the same way as the Vampire. A drafted Tombstone can face any way: dealer's choice.

Can I throw a hazard over a Tombstone?

No. Only over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.

A Vampire, Bloodwell, Tombstone or Dynamite is in the way of my throw. What happens?

You can't throw past it. That throw just isn't allowed, and nothing is damaged.

The pool is empty. Can I throw?

No. Wait until a hazard leaves the board and goes back to the pool.

A player is out. Do their hazards leave too?

No. Only their Vampires and Bloodwells leave. Hazards and Dynamite belong to the board.

Can I draft a hazard onto a Hallowed square? (Immortal)

No. After the draft, Hallowed squares don't matter.

Can a hazard go on a Bloodwell's square?

Never.

Dynamite

What sets off Dynamite?

Only a shot (any shot, including the Hand Cannon) or another blast. A thrown hazard doesn't.

All 3 sticks are on the board. Can I place another?

No. Wait until one explodes and goes back to the crate.

Does a Tombstone protect me from a blast?

No.

Can something get hit twice in one chain?

No. Each blast finishes before the next one starts, and each piece is hit only once.

Who gets credit for a chain?

The player who fired the shot that set off the first stick.

Healing

Does Bloodbath cost AP?

Never. It's free, even when Cursed.

Can I Bloodbath more than once?

Yes. Every time you end a move next to a Bloodwell.

Does it have to be my own Bloodwell?

No. Any Bloodwell works, and diagonals count.

I step onto Grave Dust next to a Bloodwell. What happens first?

The hazard goes off first, then you can Bloodbath.

Faction abilities (Deadly and Immortal)

How much is the Silver Bullet?

4 AP: 1 to load and 3 to fire, in the same turn.

Can I load the Silver Bullet this turn and fire next turn?

No. Same turn, or not at all.

Are once-per-game abilities once per Vampire?

No, once per player. Turn the card over when you use it.

Can a revived Sheriff act right away?

No. It returns healthy, facing any way you like, and waits.

Which Sheriff gets Swift Justice?

Your active Sheriff, the one you acted with this turn.

Do I have to choose when Contract Payoff triggers?

Yes. On a kill, you must either cash +2 now or raise the marker to +4. Once raised, your next kill automatically pays +4.

I took down two things with one shot. Does Contract Payoff pay twice?

No. Several kills from one command pay once. But you can raise the marker on one kill and cash +4 on a later kill in the same turn.

Can I spend Vengeance AP on someone else's turn?

Yes, right when your loss resolves. Pivot memory resets for it, and once-per-game cards work. Roll whatever's left into your next turn.

Does Vengeance trigger if I kill my own Vampire?

No. It only comes from another player.

My Vigilante AP is past 11. How do I track it?

On trust. Keep count in your head.

What's "Dust to Dust"?

The name for the Hand Cannon combo kill: a Vampire hit by the Hand Cannon shot and then by the blast it sets off. It's just a name, with no extra rules effect.

Is there a tie?

Only GHOST TOWN, when the last players all go out at once. There's no turn limit.
