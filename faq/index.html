Playtest FAQ

DRAFT. Answers pending Adam, the Manual Maker and Rules Keeper.

How long does a game take?

60 to 90 minutes. The first game, with teaching, runs closer to 90.

Which tier should we pick?

Beginner if anyone's new. Deadly if your table has played before or lives on tactics games. Immortal once Deadly feels safe. Your table decides. See the tiers.

Can I join a game already going?

Not mid-game. Seats are set at the start. Grab the next game at any table, or watch one and learn the ropes.

How many can play?

2 to 4 per table. Each faction is played by one player, first come, first served.

Which layout do we use?

Any card for your player count. Can't decide? Draw one at random.

What if we disagree on a rule?

Pick the reading that keeps the game moving and play on. Then tell us in the feedback. That's exactly what tonight is for.

Who do we ask?

Adam. He's the one in the hat.

Where do I leave feedback?

Scan the code on your table, or tap below. About 60 seconds. Knocked out early? Do it then, while it still stings.

Do I have to give my name?

No. Tick “Include me as a playtester” only if you want your name in the rulebook credits.

Is this the finished game?

No. The pieces are temporary and the rules can still change. Your notes decide how.
