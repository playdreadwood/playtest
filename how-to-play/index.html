How to play

Tonight? There's only enough blood for one.

2–4 players · ages 14+ · 60–90 minutes

Pick your tier

Each table picks one way to play before setup. Everyone learns Beginner first. Deadly and Immortal only add to it.

Beginner

The goal

Be the last player standing. You're out when all 3 of your Bloodwells are destroyed or both your Vampires are eliminated.

Setup

  1. Pick a faction. Take its 2 Vampires and 3 Bloodwells.
  2. Set up exactly as your layout card shows, facings included.
  3. All 12 hazards go in the pool. The 3 Dynamite sticks go in the crate.
  4. First player: whoever most recently saw their own blood. Play goes clockwise.

Your turn

  1. Pick one Vampire.
  2. Take 5 AP. Round 1 only: 4, 5, 6, 6 by seat.
  3. Spend every point, in any order.

Can't spend it all at the start of your turn? You're Staked, and you're out.

Actions

Move. One square forward.1 AP
Pivot. Face a new way. Not twice in a row.1 AP Shot. Straight ahead. Hits the first thing in line.3 AP Throw Hazard. 1 to 3 squares ahead, onto an empty square.1 AP Place Dynamite. On the square in front of you.3 AP Bloodbath. End a move next to any Bloodwell. Heals everything.0 AP

Health

  • Hit once: Bleeding (red ring). Hit again: eliminated.
  • Cursed (magenta ring): +1 AP per action. No throwing, no Dynamite, no Tombstones.

Shots

A shot stops at the first thing it hits. Vampires take a hit. Bloodwells are destroyed. Dynamite explodes. Shots fly over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.

A Vampire on a Tombstone is safe from shots on the side it faces. Shot from any other side, it's hit. An empty Tombstone just breaks.

Hazards

  • Tombstone: cover.
  • Bear Trap: step on it, Bleed.
  • Grave Dust: step on it, Cursed.
  • Throws can only fly over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.

Dynamite

A shot or blast sets it off. It hits all 8 squares around it, your pieces too.

Winning

Last one standing wins. Everyone out at once? Shout "GHOST TOWN!" Nobody wins.

Silver Bullet

every player · once per game · 4 AP

Load and fire in one turn. One hit eliminates.

Sheriffs

Under My Protection

passive

No bullet hurts a Bloodwell with one of your Sheriffs next to it. Dynamite still does.

Swift Justice

passive

After your turn ends, your Sheriff may move once and pivot once, free.

Order Restored

once per game · 2 AP

Bring back a dead Sheriff next to your other Sheriff or one of your Bloodwells.

Bounty Hunters

Sharpshooter

passive

Your shots pass through Tombstones and hit whoever is on them.

Marked Man

passive

A Standard Shot that hits an enemy Vampire also Curses it.

Contract Payoff

once per game

Kill an enemy Vampire or Bloodwell: take +2 AP now, or raise to +4 and cash it on a later kill. Same turn is fine.

Vigilantes

Fluid Malice

passive

Use both Vigilantes each turn, sharing one AP pool.

Bound by Blood

passive

+1 AP if your turn starts with both inside a 4×4 block.

Vengeance is Mine

reaction

Another player kills your Vampire or Bloodwell: +4 AP. Spend it now or next turn.

Outlaws

Daring Escape

passive

Destroy an enemy Bloodwell: 2 free moves and 2 free pivots, right away.

Rampage

once per game · 3 AP

Two shots at once, straight left and straight right.

Hand Cannon

once per game · 4 AP

Hits everything in the 5 squares ahead. Smashes Tombstones and keeps going.

Hazard Draft

Set up › draft › play
  • The player who goes last picks first. Then counter-clockwise.
  • Each pick: one hazard, onto an empty square that isn't Hallowed (striped on the layout card).
  • Tombstones face any way. Dealer's choice.
