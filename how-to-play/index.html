Beginner

Draft

The point

Everyone gets two vampires and three Bloodwells. Lose both vampires, or all three Bloodwells, and you're out. Last one standing wins.

Setup

  1. Pick a faction. First come, first served. Take its 2 vampires, 3 Bloodwells, AP tracker card, quick reference card and meeple.
  2. Pick a layout card for your player count, or draw one at random.
  3. Place every piece exactly as the card shows, facing included. Then the card is done.
  4. All 12 hazards go in the shared pool. The 3 dynamite wait in their own supply.
  5. First player: whoever most recently saw their own blood. Play goes clockwise.

Your turn

Choose one vampire. You get 5 AP. First round only: the first player gets 4, the next 5, anyone after that 6.

Spend every point. Exactly. No saving. Can't make it come out even? Undo until you can. Still can't? You're Staked. Out.

Move. One square, the way you face. Never diagonal.1 AP
Pivot. Face N, E, S or W. Never two in a row.1 AP
Standard Shot. Straight ahead, any distance. Stops at the first thing it hits, friends included.3 AP
Throw Hazard. Any hazard from the pool, 1 to 3 squares ahead, onto an empty square.1 AP
Place Dynamite. On the empty square in front of you. Nobody throws it.3 AP
Bloodbath. End a move next to any Bloodwell, diagonals count. Clears Bleeding and the Curse.0 AP

Getting hurt

  • First hit: Bleeding. Hit again: eliminated. A hit Bloodwell is destroyed.
  • Bear Trap: step on it and you Bleed.
  • Grave Dust: step on it and you're Cursed. Everything but Pivot and Bloodbath costs 1 more AP. No throwing, no dynamite, no Tombstones.
  • Tombstone: cover, but only from the side the vampire faces. Shots fly over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.
  • Dynamite: any shot sets it off. Hits all 8 squares around it. Friends included.

The end

Last one standing wins. If everyone goes down at once, nobody wins. But also everyone wins. Shout “Ghost Town!”

Deadly

Draft

Beginner +

All the Beginner rules, plus each faction's cards: abilities, the Silver Bullet and bonus AP.

Silver Bullet

Once per game. Load and fire in the same turn for 4 AP. A hit eliminates on the spot. A Tombstone still stops it.

The factions

Sheriffs

Guard their wells and come back. Bullets can't touch a Bloodwell with a Sheriff beside it. A free move or pivot at the end of the turn. Once per game, a dead Sheriff returns.

Outlaws

Break wells and run. Destroy an enemy Bloodwell and get a free escape. Once per game each: Rampage, two shots left and right. Hand Cannon, through everything for 5 squares.

Bounty Hunters

Shoot through your cover. Shots pass straight through Tombstones. Clean hits curse the target. Kills pay bonus AP, or bank it for a bigger payday.

Vigilantes

Move as a pair and pay you back. Both vampires act on one shared AP pool. +1 AP when they stay close. Lose a vampire or a well to someone else and gain 4 AP, right then.

Full ability text is on each faction's cards.

Immortal

Draft

Deadly +

All the Deadly rules, plus the Hazard Draft. You lay the traps yourselves.

The Hazard Draft

  1. Set up the pieces from the layout card as usual. All 12 hazards start in the pool.
  2. The player who goes last drafts first. Then counter-clockwise.
  3. On your pick, take any one hazard and place it on any empty square that isn't Hallowed ground (the striped squares on the card).
  4. Keep going until everyone has their picks. Then play.
2 players: 4 each, 4 left in the pool4
3 players: 3 each, 3 left in the pool3
4 players: 3 each, pool empty3

Dynamite is never drafted. Hallowed ground only matters during the draft.
