# Silver Bullet | var(--parch) | silver
### Silver Bullet | every player · once per game · 4 AP
1 AP to load + 3 to fire, same turn. One hit eliminates.
# Sheriffs | var(--sheriff) | sheriffs
### Under My Protection | passive
No bullet hurts your Bloodwell while one of your Sheriffs is next to it. Dynamite still does.
!D10 Under My Protection: the shot stops at the guarded well
### Swift Justice | passive
At the very end of your turn, your active Sheriff may make one free move and/or one free pivot. The move must end next to one of your own Bloodwells (any of the 8 squares around it). If it can't, it's pivot only.
### Order Restored | once per game · 3 AP
Destroy one of your Bloodwells to bring a fallen Sheriff back on an empty square orthogonally next to your surviving Sheriff or one of your own Bloodwells. It returns Bleeding and can't act this turn. Sacrificing your last Bloodwell puts you out.
# Bounty Hunters | var(--hunter) | hunters
### Sharpshooter | passive
Your shots pass through Tombstones and hit whoever is on them.
### Marked Man | passive
A Standard Shot that hits an enemy Vampire cleanly (not through a Tombstone) also Curses it.
### Contract Payoff | once per game
Kill an enemy Vampire or Bloodwell: you must take +2 AP now, or raise to +4 and get it on your next kill (same turn is fine). One payout per command.
# Vigilantes | var(--vigil) | vigilantes
### Fluid Malice | passive
Use both Vigilantes each turn, sharing one AP pool.
### Bound by Blood | passive
+1 AP if your turn starts with both inside a 4×4 block.
### Vengeance is Mine | reaction
Another player kills your Vampire or Bloodwell: +4 AP, once per turn. Spend any of it now; the rest rolls into your next turn.
# Outlaws | var(--outlaw) | outlaws
### Daring Escape | passive
Destroy an enemy Bloodwell: 2 free moves and 2 free pivots, any order, right away. Pivoting twice in a row is fine.
### Rampage | once per game · 3 AP
Two standard shots, left then right. Each resolves on its own.
### Hand Cannon | once per game · 4 AP
Hits everything in the 5 squares ahead. Smashes Tombstones and keeps going.
!D11 Hand Cannon: 5 squares, through a Tombstone
### Reckless Reach | passive
Outlaws throw every hazard 1 square further, so a throw can land up to 4 squares ahead. And, yes, that includes dynamite. An Outlaw may place Dynamite 1 or 2 squares ahead.
