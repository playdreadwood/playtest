## The goal
Be the **last player standing**. You're out when all 3 of your Bloodwells are destroyed or both your Vampires are eliminated.
!D3 How you're out
## Setup
1. Pick a faction. Take its 2 Vampires and 3 Bloodwells.
2. Set up exactly as your layout card shows, facings included.
3. All 12 hazards go in the pool. The 3 Dynamite sticks go in the crate.
4. First player: whoever most recently saw their own blood. Play goes clockwise.
!D1 A layout card next to the board it sets up
## Your turn
1. Pick **one** Vampire.
2. Take **5 AP**. Round 1 only: 4, 5, 6, 6 by seat.
3. Spend **every** point, in any order.
Can't spend it all at the start of your turn? You're **Staked**, and you're out.
!D2 A 5 AP turn: move 1, pivot 1, shoot 3
## Actions

## Health
- **Hit once:** Bleeding (red ring). **Hit again:** eliminated.
- **Cursed** (magenta ring): +1 AP per action. No throwing, no Dynamite, no Tombstones.
!D4 Bloodbath clears both rings
## Shots
A shot stops at the first thing it hits. Vampires take a hit. Bloodwells are destroyed. Dynamite explodes. Shots fly over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.

A Vampire on a Tombstone is safe from shots on the side it faces. Shot from any other side, it's hit. An empty Tombstone just breaks.
!D7 Tombstone cover: protected versus flanked
## Hazards

- **Tombstone:** cover.
- **Bear Trap:** step on it, Bleed.
- **Grave Dust:** step on it, Cursed.
- Throws can only fly over Bear Traps and Grave Dust.
!D6 Throw Hazard: 1 to 3 squares ahead
## Dynamite
A shot or blast sets it off. It hits all 8 squares around it, your pieces too.
!D8 A Dynamite blast hits the 8 squares around it
## Winning
Last one standing wins. Everyone out at once? Shout **"GHOST TOWN!"** Nobody wins.
